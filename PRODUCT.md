# Tigermom

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Convex database, backend, auth, and static hosting; GitHub for code. Frontend framework delegated by the user: React and Vite for a phone-friendly web app.

## Users

First-time pregnant women in India who want to understand their care. Starting example: a 28-year-old in Bengaluru, eight weeks pregnant and overwhelmed after an appointment. Not an age restriction.

## Product Purpose

Help a woman understand her doctor's advice or written reports, feel supported, and prepare three questions for her care provider.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Voice or typing; a private guest session opens from the Start button with no phone number or code; optional PDF or report photo. Free conversation and report content deleted at session end. Guest sign-in keys stay in page memory. Paid membership coming soon, without payments. Future membership saves reports and history. No appointment booking, WhatsApp, or diet tracking in v1. AI processing/costs require user approval and configuration; not yet connected. No SMS service is needed.

## Brand Commitments

Empathy, empowering, woman, midwife, clarifying, simplifying. A midwife's calm without claiming to be a clinician. Indian context. Verified evidence, attributed childbirth-education perspectives, relevant news only. Never fabricate claims or references. Explain uncertainty calmly and direct urgent concerns to care.

## Evidence on Hand

IDEA_SCOPE.md is the approved scope. Founder can reach three women for testing alongside the build. No testimonials, clinical validation, or usage claims supplied.

## Product Principles

Empathy before evidence. Agency before instructions. Plain language. Verified sources. Private free sessions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Typing for women who cannot speak privately; phone-first interaction and readable, keyboard-accessible controls.
